Privacy Policy

Skyferry · Last updated: November 14, 2026

This policy explains what information the mobile game Skyferry (the "Game") uses, why, and the choices you have. The Game is operated by R² Studio ("we", "us"), based in Israel. If you have questions, write to r2.studio777@gmail.com.

1. The short version

  • You do not need an account. We do not ask for your name, email, phone number, location or contacts.
  • Your progress (level, coins, lives, settings) is stored on your device.
  • The Game is free and shows ads. Our advertising partner collects some device information to show and measure ads (see section 3).
  • Purchases, if offered, are handled by Apple or Google. We never see your payment card.
  • You can erase the data on your device by uninstalling the Game, or ask us about anything else by email.

3. Ads and our advertising partner

The Game earns money from advertising. The Game uses Google AdMob (Google LLC / Google Ireland Limited), and possibly other ad networks working through it. When an ad is requested or shown, the ad partner may automatically collect:

  • Your device's advertising identifier (Android Advertising ID or Apple IDFA, where allowed and where you have permitted it);
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it;
  • Device and app information (device model, operating system and version, language, app version, time zone, network type);
  • Ad interaction data (ads shown, clicked, watched).

They use this to show ads, limit how often you see the same ad, measure performance, prevent fraud, and, where you have consented if required, to personalise ads. Third parties, including Google and its partners, may place or read cookies and similar technologies, or use web beacons, device identifiers or IP addresses, as a result of ad serving in the Game. Learn more in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and in Google's Privacy Policy. Google acts as an independent data controller for the ad data it collects. If we add other ad networks, we will list them here.

Your choices:

  • On your device, you can limit ad personalisation in your system ad settings (see below).
  • In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, we ask for your consent before personalised ads (through a consent form), and you can change your choice at any time through the same form when it is offered, or by contacting us.
  • On iPhone: you decide in the system prompt whether the Game may track you across apps, and you can change it in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. On Android: you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ads personalisation, in Settings → Privacy (or Google) → Ads.
  • Resetting your advertising ID makes data previously linked to it no longer connected to you.

4. What we do not do

  • We do not sell your personal information in exchange for money. Note: under some laws (for example in California), sharing an advertising ID with ad partners for personalised ads can count as "selling" or "sharing". You can opt out any time by limiting ad personalisation in your device settings or in the consent form (see section 3).
  • We do not ask for accounts, real names, contacts, photos, microphone or precise location.
  • We do not make decisions about you by automated means that have legal or similarly significant effects.
  • We do not run our own analytics or crash-reporting service. (If we add one in the future, we will update this policy and, where required, ask for your consent first.)

5. Children

The Game is intended for users aged 13 and over (or the higher minimum age for digital consent in your country, which is up to 16 in some countries) and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children below that age. Parents can use their device's ad settings to limit ad personalisation. If you are a parent and believe your child has given us personal information, contact us at r2.studio777@gmail.com and we will delete it. In-app purchases by children should be made only with a parent's permission.

6. Legal bases (EEA / UK users)

Where the GDPR applies, we rely on: contract (to provide the Game and save your progress); consent (for personalised ads and use of the advertising identifier - you can withdraw it at any time); and legitimate interests (basic ad delivery, fraud prevention and keeping the Game secure).

7. Sharing and transfers

We share information only with: our ad partners (section 3); Apple/Google for purchases and app distribution; and authorities when the law requires it. Some partners are located outside your country, including in the United States and Israel. Where required, transfers rely on lawful mechanisms such as the EU-US Data Privacy Framework or standard contractual clauses.

8. Retention

Game data stays on your device until you uninstall the Game or clear its data. We keep nothing on our own servers. Ad partners keep their data under their own policies.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live (including under the GDPR, UK GDPR, California law, and Israel's Protection of Privacy Law), you may have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to processing of your personal information, to withdraw consent, and to complain to a data-protection authority (in Israel, the Privacy Protection Authority). Because we do not hold an account or your identity, most of what we hold is on your device and you can erase it by uninstalling the Game or clearing its data. For anything else, email r2.studio777@gmail.com. We will reply within a reasonable time and within the period the law requires.

10. Security

We keep the information we handle to a minimum and use reasonable measures to protect it. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

11. Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version. If a change is significant, we will tell you in the Game or on the store page before it takes effect.
